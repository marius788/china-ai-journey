# AI Engineering Abroad 🇨🇳

## My First Two Weeks at SZTU

The first two weeks of my semester abroad at **Shenzhen Technology University (SZTU)** have now come to an end.

I have been really impressed by the **speed at which technology is developing and being adopted in China**. Walking through Shenzhen, you can already see technologies that still feel futuristic in many other places — self-driving cars, AI glasses, drones delivering goods, and many other applications of AI in everyday life.

One of the main reasons I decided to come to China was to experience a new culture, but also to **see first-hand why technology and AI are developing and being adopted so quickly here**.

### My Courses

During my semester, I am taking a mix of technical and business-oriented courses:

- 🤖 **LLM Applications**
- 🧠 **Reinforcement Learning**
- 🦾 **Tactile Robotics**
- 👤 **Personal Branding**
- 💼 **Business Simulation**

I am especially interested in the combination of **AI, robotics, and real-world applications**.

I hope to learn as much as possible over the coming weeks — not only through the courses themselves, but also by experiencing the technology ecosystem here in Shenzhen.
