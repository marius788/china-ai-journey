## Highlights

- Arrival in Shenzhen, accommodation, campus orientation at SZTU
- Course kickoffs and getting to know the semester setup
- First walks through the city: self-driving cars, AI glasses, delivery
  drones — technology as everyday life, not as a trade-fair demo

## Course takeaways

- **All courses:** overview of syllabus and expectations; choosing where to
  go deep (AI × robotics × real-world applications).
- **Personal Branding:** decision to document the semester publicly in
  English — this portfolio.

## Tech observations

The speed of adoption stands out most: things that feel futuristic elsewhere
are simply *in use* here. My working question for the semester: **what makes
the path from lab to street so short in Shenzhen?**

## Reflection

Week 1 was orientation, not depth. Goal for week 2: go from "impressive" to
specific — one concrete observation per course, per week.
