## Highlights

- First full lecture week: LLM Applications, Reinforcement Learning,
  Tactile Robotics

## Course takeaways

- **Business Simulation:** understanding the company, the markets and the
  decision rhythm; first practice rounds to learn the mechanics before the
  real rounds count.
- **LLM Applications:** from chatbots to agents — LLMs that use tools and
  plan multi-step tasks. First project ideas collected.
- **Reinforcement Learning:** agents learning from reward — the conceptual
  bridge between the simulation (decisions under uncertainty) and robotics.
- **Tactile Robotics:** first contact with the lab side of AI — sensing and
  manipulation.
